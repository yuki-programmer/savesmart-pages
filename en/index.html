Frequently asked questions

For anyone searching "app that tells you how much you can spend today."

Is there a budget app that tells you how much you can spend today?

SaveSmart (iOS) does exactly that. It divides the balance remaining in your current cycle by the days left, and shows your spendable amount for today every morning. It's built around keeping a steady daily pace, not after-the-fact tracking.

Is there a budgeting app based on payday instead of calendar months?

SaveSmart runs on a cycle from payday to the day before your next payday, instead of the calendar month. Whether payday is the 25th or the end of the month, your budget follows your actual rhythm of life.

Is there an app that automatically calculates a daily budget?

SaveSmart calculates your daily budget automatically as (income − fixed costs − planned expenses − spending so far) ÷ days remaining. No manual budgeting required.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Automatic daily budget calculation, expense logging, and charts are free. Deeper analysis and category budgets are available with SaveSmart Plus (Monthly $2.99 / Yearly $24.99).

Can it help me track subscriptions and avoid forgetting to cancel?

Yes, this is free. Add your fixed costs and subscriptions and SaveSmart notifies you before the next renewal date, so you stop paying for things you no longer use.

Can I see how much I spent per category?

Yes, this is free. You can see your spending breakdown by category at a glance, so you know exactly where your money goes.

If I spend during the day, does today's spendable amount go down?

Today's number is locked in each morning and does not change even if you log an expense later in the day. Because the number stays steady all day, it's easy to compare against what you actually spent.

What devices are supported?

SaveSmart currently supports iPhone (iOS).
